Important safety warning

Rouzey supports nitric-oxide production, which affects blood flow and blood pressure. Do not use Rouzey if you take nitrate medication (such as nitroglycerin), erectile dysfunction medication, or blood pressure medication without talking to your doctor first. Combining these with a nitric-oxide supplement can cause an unsafe drop in blood pressure. If you have a heart condition or a history of low blood pressure, talk to your doctor before use. If you feel faint, dizzy, or unwell after taking Rouzey, stop use and seek medical attention.

Always

  • Short sentences. One idea each.
  • Verbs over adjectives ("get blood flowing").
  • Numbers and specifics (600 mg, 15 minutes).
  • Say what we don't do, plainly.
  • One italic accent phrase per page.

Never

  • "Powerful," "revolutionary," "game-changing."
  • Gradient text, glows, neon, dark-mode hype.
  • Blush pink; any second accent color.
  • Fake urgency, fake reviews, fake counts.
  • Gym or medical-clinic language.

Motion & access

Motion is subtle and earns its place: fade-up reveals, the smart-hide header, card lift on selection. Everything switches off under prefers-reduced-motion. Floor: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible focus rings in bordeaux, full keyboard operability, skip link.
